Expert Boat & PWC Service in Clearfield, Utah

A great day on the water starts with a safe, reliable journey to the boat ramp. At Dick's Boat Shop, we proudly offer premium Voyager Trailers to ensure your haul is as smooth as your ride. Engineered for maximum durability and precision-fit for a wide variety of watercraft, Voyager trailers are built to withstand the elements and tackle Utah's diverse terrain. Whether you need a secure ride for your fresh new WaveRunner or a heavy-duty bunk trailer for your tournament fishing boat, our team will help you find the perfect match for your tow vehicle and vessel.

Explore Our Trailer Categories

Single PWC Trailers

Lightweight and Effortless Trailering

Perfectly balanced for a single personal watercraft, these trailers offer a compact footprint that is easy to tow with smaller SUVs or crossovers. They feature low-profile designs for seamless launching even at shallow boat ramps.

Tandem (Dual) PWC Trailers

Double the Fun, One Tow Vehicle

Designed for families with multiple machines, our tandem WaveRunner trailers let you haul two personal watercraft side-by-side with perfect weight distribution. Heavy-duty frames and walking tracks make loading and securing both units a breeze.

Fishing & Aluminum Boat Trailers

Rugged Support for the Avid Angler

Built to handle the specific dimensions of aluminum fishing hulls and utility boats. These trailers offer optimized hull support to prevent flexing during long highway hauls to your favorite remote fishing holes.

Fiberglass Boat Trailers

Premium Defense for High-Performance Hulls

Engineered to carry heavier fiberglass sport and bass boats with elite structural support. These trailers feature plush, carpeted bunks to protect your gelcoat, alongside upgraded braking systems for confident stopping power.

Built with adjustable bunks and winch stands to perfectly cradle your specific hull design.

Corrosion Resistance

Features heavy-duty galvanized or premium powder-coated finishes to resist rust and road debris.

Smooth Towing
