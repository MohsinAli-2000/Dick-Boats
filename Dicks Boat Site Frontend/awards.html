Our Awards

Excellence Earned, Generations Trusted

At Dick's Boat Shop, we don't measure our success by the number of sales tags we hang, but by the smiles on our customers' faces when they head out to the lake. Because we treat every customer like family, our commitment to honest service has earned us some of the highest honors in both the local Utah business community and the national marine industry.

While we are proud of these trophies, we know they are earned every single day at the service counter and on the showroom floor.
