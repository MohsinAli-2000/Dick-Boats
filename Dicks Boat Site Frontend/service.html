Our Core Marine Services

No job is too big or too small. As a full-service facility, we offer a comprehensive suite of maintenance and repair options

Routine Scheduled Maintenance

Oil changes, filter replacements, spark plug swaps, and multi-point safety inspections to prevent problems before they start.

Advanced Diagnostics & Engine Repair

Specialized troubleshooting for inboard, outboard, and PWC engines to trace electrical, fuel system, or mechanical issues.

Winterization

Don't let Utah's freezing winters crack your block. We offer full system winterization, fluid stabilization, and shrink-wrapping.

Spring Summarization

De-winterization, battery testing, systems checks, and fire-up so you are 100% ready for opening day.

Trailer Repairs & Inspections

Accessory & Electronics Installation

Professional installation of fishfinders, GPS navigation, trolling motors, and marine audio upgrades.
