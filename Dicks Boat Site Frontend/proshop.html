Welcome to the Pro Shop at Dick's Boat Shop

Gear up for peak performance both on and off the water. The Dick's Boat Shop Pro Shop is Northern Utah's go-to destination for premium watersports gear, factory-authorized marine parts, and high-quality accessories designed to maximize your time on the lake. We don't just outfit your vessel; we outfit your entire adventure. By partnering exclusively with the industry's most trusted brands, we ensure you have access to the safest, most durable, and highest-performing equipment on the market. Drop by our Clearfield showroom or explore our curated categories below to find exactly what you need for your next epic lake day.

What's in Store

Watersports & Towables

Engineered for Maximum Fun

Catch the ultimate ride with our selection of multi-rider tubes, wakeboards, wakesurfers, and heavy-duty tow ropes. Whether you're teaching the kids to ski or dialing in a new trick, we have the gear to upgrade your summer.

Premium Life Jackets & Safety Gear

Protection You Can Trust

Stay safe on Utah's waterways with US Coast Guard-approved life jackets for infants, kids, and adults. We carry lightweight, comfortable nylon and neoprene vests alongside essential safety kits, fire extinguishers, and throw cushions.

Genuine Yamaha Parts & Yamalube Care

Protect Your Investment with OEM Quality

Keep your machine running like new. We stock genuine Yamaha outboard and WaveRunner components, including Yamalube oils, fuel stabilizers, oil filters, and spark plugs for routine maintenance or quick repairs.

Marine Electronics & Navigation

Upgrade Your Helm

Navigate with confidence and find more fish. Explore the latest in marine technology, featuring high-definition fishfinders, GPS chartplotters, and advanced trolling motors to map your favorite local reservoirs.

Boat Covers, Anchors & Docking Gear

Secure and Shield Your Vessel

Protect your hull from the elements and keep it safe at the slip. We offer heavy-duty custom boat covers, durable marine fenders (bumpers), high-strength dock lines, and anchors engineered to hold firm in choppy waters.

Apparel, Gear Bags & Accessories

Rep Your Favorite Brands

Look the part on and off the water. Browse our collection of official Yamaha and WaveRunner branded apparel, including UV-protective rash guards, performance hats, and waterproof gear bags to keep your valuables bone-dry.
