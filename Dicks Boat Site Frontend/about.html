Our Story

Generations of Trust on the Water

At Dick's Boat Shop, boating isn't just a business—it's our family legacy. As the oldest active marine dealer in the state of Utah, we have spent over six decades helping families create unforgettable memories on the water and in the mountains. Our roots are humble, our passion is deep, and our commitment to our community remains exactly as it was the day we opened.

1959. The Garage Days & A Blueprint for the Future

Our story began in 1959 in a small garage in Clearfield, Utah. While working full-time at Hill Air Force Base and having recently served his country in the Korean War, our grandfather, Richard "Dick" Weeks, launched a side gig and a backup plan. He started out selling Johnson outboard motors and unique "build-a-boat" kits—where customers would buy a boat in a crate and assemble it right at home. Upon his retirement from Hill Air Force Base, Dick turned his side passion into his full-time career. By 1969, the business had outgrown the garage, prompting a move to our current location to focus heavily on dedicated marine service and boat sales. Over the decades, we've proudly represented legendary boat lines like Glastron, Sunbird, and Century.

The Second Generation

A Lifelong Legacy

In 1960, Dick's youngest son, Tracy Weeks, was born. Tracy quite literally grew up in the business. As a kid, the "boat yard" was his playground, as he spent his days running between hulls and outboards. That lifelong immersion turned into a career of unmatched expertise. Today, Tracy is the active main owner of Dick's Boat Shop. He represents a true wealth of knowledge and expertise in the marine industry, ensuring that the foundational values of honesty and top-tier service remain intact every single day.

The Evolution of a 50-Year Yamaha Partnership

To keep the shop busy during Utah's brisk winters, Dick made a move that would change the trajectory of our business forever: he started selling snowmobiles. That winter hustle introduced us to Yamaha, sparking a premier partnership that has lasted nearly 50 years.

As Yamaha grew, we grew with them

The Outboard & Generator Era

We proudly added Yamaha outboards and power products to our lineup.

1986 - The WaveRunner Revolution

When Yamaha introduced "Water Vehicles" (now known as WaveRunners), Dick immediately recognized their game-changing potential. To accommodate this exploding personal watercraft (PWC) market, we built our current facility in 1989.

The 1990s - Expanding the Fleet

When Yamaha introduced their wildly popular jet sport boats, we jumped on board. Around the same time, Yamaha acquired Skeeter Boats and G3 Boats, allowing us to offer world-class fiberglass bass boats and durable aluminum fishing boats to our local anglers.

Note: While our brands have evolved, we haven't forgotten where we came from. OMC (now BRP) is our longest-standing partner. Even though they no longer manufacture new outboards, we remain a dedicated service, parts, and accessories center for BRP products.

Remembering a Titan & Looking Forward

In 2015, we suffered a heartbreaking loss when our founder, Dick, passed away after an illness. He was a true titan of the marine industry, and his decades of wisdom entirely shaped who we are today. Dick's guiding philosophy was simple: stay small, remain accommodating, and always value the customer. He always said: "Large enough to accommodate, small enough to appreciate." Fast forward to today, and that philosophy still guides us. While we take pride in our small, personal, independent stature, our achievements are massive: We are the oldest active marine dealer in Utah. We consistently rank at the top of national sales for Yamaha Sport Boats and WaveRunners. We have been recognized with factory Customer Satisfaction Awards year after year. We have so much to look forward to. From our family to yours, we can't wait to help you gear up, get out there, and go have fun on the water once more.
